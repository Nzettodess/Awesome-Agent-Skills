## 📚 Skills Catalog

### 💼 Career
- **cv-writer**: Craft and structure CVs.
- **professional-proofreader**: Proofreading for career documents.
- **rendercv**: LaTeX/YAML-based CV generator (`rendercv/rendercv`).
- **review-resume**: Resume review and feedback.

## 🚀 Usage

These skills are designed to be integrated directly into your AI agent's environment. To use them:

1. **Clone the repository**: Download the collection of skills to your local workspace.
2. **Install dependencies**: Ensure any required dependencies for specific skills are installed.
3. **Configure your agent**: Point your AI agent to the specific skill directories you wish to enable.

*Note: Each skill may contain its own specific `README.md` or `SKILL.md` with further instructions.*
